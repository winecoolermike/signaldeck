How signals work

Every number on this page is computed by build_data.py from Yahoo Finance data (via the open-source yfinance library). Nothing is estimated or filled in by hand — if a value can't be computed, it shows —. Rules are deliberately simple and fixed; they describe the current state of price, they do not predict it.

Pullback setup (the only rule that held up out of sample)

A green SETUP: Pullback badge appears on a card only when, on the latest completed daily close (split- and dividend-adjusted bars, as in the research):

  • close > SMA(200) of daily closes, and
  • RSI(2, Wilder) < 10.

Grade A if the stock is also in the top 10 of the 23-stock universe by 12-1 month momentum, otherwise grade B. 12-1 momentum = close 21 trading days ago ÷ close 252 trading days ago − 1 (the last month is skipped), on adjusted closes. The ranking is taken at the last completed month-end close and used until the next one; on the last trading day of a month the new ranking applies from that day's close (same convention as the research). Stocks without 12 months of history are unranked (so ranks are currently "of 22"). ADX(14) is still shown, for information only. The universe is the 7 dashboard tickers plus SPY, QQQ, IWM, AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, JPM, XOM, JNJ, PG, KO, WMT, HD, CAT, UNH, NEE; all 23 are on the Scanner.

Why momentum instead of ADX: in round-2 testing (2005–22 to choose, 2023 → now to score) the top-10-momentum filter raised net per trade and cut the worst trade while keeping the hit rate; its figures (from setups.json) are in the card's table and on Backtest → Setups. Honest caveat: its p-value cleared the multiple-testing bar in force when it was tested, but against the stricter bar after round 3's extra tests it narrowly misses (exact numbers on the backtest page). On the 7 dashboard tickers alone it was not significant.

Exit rule: the first daily close above SMA(5), or after 10 trading days, whichever comes first. Long only: no PUT/short setups and no intraday setups survived testing. The card also shows an optional 3×ATR(14) disaster-stop price (not part of the rule; it cut the worst losses but lowered the average) and a size hint based on the out-of-sample loss sizes. When the setup is not firing, the card shows a muted line with the current RSI(2), SMA200 position and momentum rank, so you can see how close it is.

The badge's statistics are frozen out-of-sample results (setups.json, derived from the research result files by make_setups.py) — see Backtest → Setups. Each ticker's own line ("X itself") is recomputed on every build with the same fixed rule over 2023 → latest close; fewer than 30 trades is flagged "too few to trust". The rule parameters live in signals.py (SETUP) and are never re-tuned by the scheduled job. Losses ran bigger than wins, results are for stock moves after 0.05%/side costs (not options), and past results don't predict future ones.

1. Trend state per timeframe (rows Main, 60m, 30m, 15m, 5m, 1m)

The table is labelled "Trend state, not a forecast": it describes the trend so far and does not predict the rest of the day. Out of sample (2023–26, 23 stocks) the daily version was right about as often as chance (exact figures under the table). Don't read it as a prediction, and don't invert it either (fading high-confidence readings also failed). Rows use regular-session bars only.

Each row uses the indicator that described its timeframe best in testing (round 3, below):

RowWhat it showsScore in Total
Main, 60m, 30mCALL / PUT / NEUT from the 4-vote score below (unchanged; on 60m/30m nothing tested beat it).s (−1…+1)
15mUp if EMA(9) > EMA(21) of completed 15-minute closes, Down if below, with "since HH:MM ET" = end of the first bar of the current run. EMAs run continuously across sessions.+1 / −1
5m, 1mAbove / Below VWAP: last completed bar's close vs the session VWAP (typical price (H+L+C)/3 × volume, summed from 09:30 ET). The 5m row shows the distance in %. Before today's first bar closes it shows waiting for first bar and is left out of Total. An exact tie shows "At VWAP" and scores 0.+1 / −1

The bar still forming is never used on the 15m/5m/1m rows. Outside market hours they show the last session's state.

Small-sample caveats: Yahoo only serves ~60 days of 15m/5m bars and ~7 days of 1m bars, so the scored ("test") windows are 30 sessions for 15m/5m and 10 for 1m. These are descriptive comparisons, not trading results. "Rest of day right" near the base rate means none of the rows forecasts where the day closes.

4-vote score (Main, 60m, 30m). For each of those timeframes four indicators each cast a vote of +1 (bullish), −1 (bearish) or 0:

ComponentRuleWeight
EMA trend crossEMA(20) > EMA(50) → +1, otherwise −1. EMA uses α = 2/(n+1).30%
Long trendClose > SMA(200) of that timeframe → +1, otherwise −1.30%
Momentum zoneRSI(14, Wilder) > 55 → +1, < 45 → −1, 45–55 → 0.20%
MACDMACD(12,26,9) histogram > 0 → +1, < 0 → −1.20%

Score s = Σ(weight × vote) / Σ(weights of available components), so −1 ≤ s ≤ +1. A component needs enough bars (EMA cross 50, SMA200 200, RSI 30, MACD 35); if there aren't enough, it is skipped, the remaining weights are renormalised and the card lists it under "Data notes".

Bias: s ≥ +0.25 → CALL; s ≤ −0.25 → PUT; otherwise NEUT.
Confidence: CALL/PUT → 50 + 50·|s| (50–100%). NEUT → 50 + 50·(1 − |s|/0.25), i.e. 100% when the votes cancel exactly, ~50% when it's about to tip.

Trend-strength tag next to the Main/60m/30m rows is ADX(14, Wilder) on that timeframe: L < 20 ≤ OK < 25 ≤ H < 40 ≤ V.H. It is informational and does not change the bias. On the 15m/5m/1m rows that column shows the row's score (+1/−1).

2. Total row & overall trend label

Total score = weighted average of the row scores: Main (daily) 30%, 60m 20%, 30m 15%, 15m 15%, 5m 10%, 1m 10% (renormalised if a row is missing or waiting). The 15m/5m/1m rows contribute +1 or −1. Same bias/confidence thresholds (±0.25) as above. The trend label is Bullish / Bearish / Neutral from the Total bias and "Trend ~X%" = |Total score| × 100. Like the rows, it describes the trend so far and does not predict the rest of the day.

3. Main daily signal score (0–100)

Daily score = 50 + 50 × sdaily. Labels: ≥ 70 Strong CALL, 58–69 Moderate CALL, 43–57 Neutral, 31–42 Moderate PUT, ≤ 30 Strong PUT.

4. Entry & exit levels (daily bars)

Direction follows the sign of the Total score (≥ 0 = long/CALL idea, < 0 = short/PUT idea). ATR = ATR(14, Wilder) on daily bars; C = last daily close.

Prior-day high / low / close and ATR are shown for reference. Percentages next to levels are distance from C.

5. Trade quality meter (0–100)

Quality = 40% × timeframe agreement + 30% × trend strength + 30% × options liquidity, where

  • Agreement = |Σ wtf·signtf| / Σ wtf (CALL / Up / Above = +1, PUT / Down / Below = −1, NEUT = 0). 1.0 = all timeframes point the same way.
  • Trend strength = min(daily ADX / 40, 1).
  • Options liquidity = max(0, 1 − avg relative spread / 20%), using the best OTM call & put of the 3 nearest expiries. Relative spread = (ask − bid) / mid. No usable quotes → 0.

Labels: < 30 Poor, 30–54 Fair, 55–74 Good, ≥ 75 Excellent.

6. Options panel — what "best" means

For each expiry up to 35 days out, we look at the 3 nearest out-of-the-money strikes (calls: strike > last price, puts: strike < last price). A contract is eligible only if bid > 0, ask > 0 and open interest or volume > 0. The "best" is the eligible strike with the tightest relative spread (ties → nearest strike). DTE = calendar days from the build date to expiry.

Tabs filter expiries by DTE: 0 = nearest listed expiry (0DTE when one exists); 3D = 0–3 days; 7D = 0–7 days; 1W = 4–10 days (roughly next week's expiry); 21D = 8–21 days; 30D = 22–35 days. Up to 3 expiries per tab are shown. Option quotes reflect the last session; weekend/after-hours bid/ask can be stale or wide.

7. Data caveats

  • Yahoo Finance is unofficial and quotes may be delayed ~15 min; intraday history limits: 1m ≈ last 7 days, 5m/15m/30m ≈ 60 days, 60m ≈ 730 days.
  • When markets are closed, all values are from the most recent session (timestamp shown on each card).
  • Newly listed tickers may lack 200 daily bars, so the daily SMA200 vote is skipped.
